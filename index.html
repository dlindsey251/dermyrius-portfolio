About

I work on both sides of the sentence.

I'm a writer and developer focused on interactive storytelling. My work sits between narrative design, fiction, and game development, with a particular interest in turning narrative concepts into mechanics.

I approach game writing from both sides of implementation: what a story needs to communicate and what the player actually needs to do for that story to work.
